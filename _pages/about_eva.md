Eva Valentine is a civil and environmental engineer and West Virginia University alumna (May 2026), currently working at Pennoni. She is interested in water resources and environmental stewardship, with a focus on agricultural nutrient management, watershed data analysis, and hydraulic systems design.

As an undergraduate research assistant, Eva contributed to early-stage research scoping public agricultural datasets relevant to the Chesapeake Bay Watershed, where her work included evaluating, profiling, and organizing data into subcategories relevant to poultry waste management in the region. Looking ahead, she plans to continue pursuing environmental remediation and water resources work through her current role in highway hydraulics and water systems engineering. Outside of work, she enjoys reading and exploring new cities.
