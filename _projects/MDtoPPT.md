# Streamline Your Presentation Workflow: Markdown to PowerPoint in Seconds

**Finally, a developer-friendly solution for creating professional presentations without the PowerPoint headache.**

If you're tired of wrestling with slide layouts while trying to focus on your content, we've built something for you. **Markdown-to-PowerPoint generator** transforms plain text outlines into polished presentations with a single click.

## How It Works

Write your entire slideshow in Markdown using the structured template. The VBA macro parses your `.md` file, automatically creating title slides, bullet points, comparison tables, and even splitting oversized slides into manageable chunks. No more clicking through endless formatting menus—just clean, consistent slides that look professional every time.

## Perfect For Modern Workflows

**Educators and Trainers**: Create course materials that can be version-controlled, collaborated on via Git, and regenerated instantly when content changes. Update your syllabus in Markdown and rebuild all lecture slides in seconds.

**Technical Teams**: Document architecture decisions, sprint reviews, or project updates in plain text, then generate stakeholder-ready presentations. Your content lives in readable, searchable text files alongside your codebase.

**Content Creators**: Build presentation templates once, then focus purely on messaging. The separation of content from design means you can iterate on structure without losing formatting work.

## Smart Features

The tool handles slide splitting—presentations with more than 10 bullet points automatically become multi-part slides with consistent naming. Tables convert seamlessly, and bold emphasis carries through to the final slides. Everything from 5-minute lightning talks to hour-long workshops fits the same template structure.

## Open Source \& Extensible

Available on GitHub with full source code. Customize the formatting, add new slide types, or integrate with your existing content pipeline. Because it's just VBA and Markdown, you can modify everything to match your brand guidelines or presentation standards.
